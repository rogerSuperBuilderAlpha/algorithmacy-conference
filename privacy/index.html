Privacy Conference and Studio

What we do with personal information.

This notice covers algorithmacy.org, including the conference pages and Algorithmacy Studio. It describes the information the site actually collects, who receives it, and how to ask us to delete it.

01

Who runs this site.

The Algorithmacy Conference is convened by Roger Hunt and hosted by the Caribbean Emergence Institute in Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago. Studio is a separate service on the same site, offered under the name Algorithmacy: an engineer sets up agents for an owner-run office and manages them. Registering for the conference does not start a Studio engagement, and a Studio inquiry does not register you for the conference.

Conference questions, including a request about a submission or a registration, go to rhunt@bentley.edu. A Studio inquiry, and a request to delete a Studio inquiry, goes to topanga@algorithmacy.org.

02

Studio inquiries.

The form at the bottom of /studio collects the office type (and whether you want the office engagement or other work), your name, the business, your email, a phone number if you give one, and the chore or message. It also records that you agreed to the terms and to this notice. The form does not send unless that box is checked. A hidden field is there for spam. If that field is filled in, the note is discarded and nothing is sent. The server also ignores a form that is submitted too quickly, or too often, so automated posts are less likely to get through.

We use what you submit only to respond and to follow up about the Studio service. We do not sell it, and we do not add it to a mailing list. Sending the form does not create a contract.

The form is emailed to topanga@algorithmacy.org from this site, through Hostinger Email. Your email address is written at the top of that message, because that is how we reply. There is no separate form company in the middle. You can send the same note yourself by emailing that address. The email link under the form only opens your own mail program. This site does not receive that copy unless you send it.

03

Paper submissions.

The form at /submit collects the title, authors and affiliations, a contact email, the submission type, the track, keywords, the abstract, an outline, author bios, a contribution statement, a conflict statement, and a special-session note if you add one.

We email a one-time confirmation link to that contact address through Mailgun. Until you confirm, the submission is held in Upstash Redis for one hour and then deleted. We do not publish it before you confirm.

When you confirm, the submission — including the contact email — is published as a pull request on the public GitHub repository rogerSuperBuilderAlpha/algorithmacy-conference. Reviews are public and signed. There is no anonymized stage. That is the intake the conference describes on the submission page.

We also store a hash of the contact email, not the address itself, so a later submission from the same address can update that pull request. The address itself remains in the public pull request. A separate rate-limit record, also a hash of the email, and a short-lived record of the IP address, expire after one hour. They exist to slow repeated form posts.

04

Registration.

Register Now leaves this site and opens a form hosted by Jotform. We do not run that form on this server. Jotform collects what you type there, and we receive the responses in order to register you for the conference. Fees are not yet set on this site, and this site does not take payment. Jotform’s own privacy notice applies to the form platform.

05

Email, logs, and what we do not collect.

If you email either address above, we keep the correspondence so we can answer it. The site is hosted on Vercel. Logs for the form endpoints can include the IP address and the time of the request. We use those logs to operate the site.

This site does not run analytics, does not use advertising cookies, and does not ask you to create an account. The cookie notice says what that means in the browser.

06

How long we keep it.

An unconfirmed submission expires after one hour. Rate-limit records for paper submissions expire after one hour. A published pull request stays public for as long as the repository keeps it. A Studio inquiry stays in the mailbox at topanga@algorithmacy.org while we are responding or following up. The Studio form also keeps a short-lived count of recent submissions, in the server’s memory, to slow repeats. That count is not the inquiry itself.

Mailgun, Jotform, GitHub, Upstash, Vercel, and Hostinger keep their own logs under their own rules. We do not control those retention periods, and this page does not restate them. This page does not claim a privacy or security certification.

07

Asking to see or delete it.

Email rhunt@bentley.edu for conference information. For a Studio inquiry, email topanga@algorithmacy.org. You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. A request to delete Studio form data goes to that same Studio address.

We will delete or correct what we still hold: a pending submission, a rate-limit record, a mailbox thread, a registration response we received. A pull request that is already public is different. We can close it or edit what we control. Forks, review comments, and GitHub history can remain. We will tell you when that is the case.

The conference and Studio are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

If this notice changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
