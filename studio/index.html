Who this is for.

Owner-run offices of about 5 to 50 people, where the week disappears into admin and follow-up. The fit, in order: law firms, then dental practices and med spas, real estate offices, and CPA firms. Questions worth asking first.

Where does our information go, and what do you need access to?

The engineer needs to see how the chore moves through the office: which tools, which steps, who touches the work. What access that takes, and what stays inside your systems, depends on the office. Bring any privacy or access rules you already follow. We talk through them before anyone connects anything. This page does not claim a security certification.

Can’t I just use ChatGPT?

You can. Tools in that range are the first column above: about $200 a month, and your time to run them. Studio is the other arrangement. An engineer learns the office, sets agents around the chores, and keeps them matched to the work. You prompt the engineer. You are not the one sitting with the tools.

What happens when something breaks?

Agents miss steps, and offices change. When that happens, you tell the engineer. Looking at it and adapting the swarm is what the monthly work is. This page does not promise that nothing will break, or a response window. How you reach the engineer is part of the conversation before the work starts.

How long is this, and how do we stop?

The shape is $5,000 to set the swarm up, then $1,000 a month while an engineer manages and adapts it. Length, and how you end it, are agreed before the work starts. This page does not set a term.

What is included in the $5,000?

Learning how this office actually moves work, and setting up agents around the chores you name — intake, chasing documents, follow-ups, reminders, review replies. It is not a generic install. The $1,000 a month after that is the engineer managing and adapting those agents as the office changes. What gets built first follows the chore you describe. Timing is something we talk through, not a date promised here.
